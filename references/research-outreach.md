# Research & outreach — evidence before copy

Read current `PROJECT-SETUP.md` first: engagement type, agent policy, destinations, sender, language/tone, cleared cases, channels, review gates and budgets. The client may change the chain and defaults at setup. Use authorized agents only; preparation of copy does not authorize sending messages. Existing clients (hot) and inbound requests default to a **validated dossier**, with expansion options or call prep only on request, and **no cold T1–T5 chain**. Never criticise their team or current work; frame offers as extra capacity/next step. Deliver in the user's language; retain original source titles/quotes.

## 1. Research waves
- For multiple entities, assign small disjoint batches (≈10 companies per agent), exact company IDs, questions, source priorities, output paths and stop budget. For one company/product/site use the dimensions below. Observe per-tool caps in `references/tooling-limits.md`.
- Record each fact with source URL, publication/event date and access date; use UNKNOWN when absent. Separate CONFIRMED primary-source facts from SECONDARY reports and INFERRED needs. Link talk/post/interview substance, with timestamp or section where available.
- Prefer official brand/product text, first-person talks/posts/interviews and current announcements. Attribute secondary/self-reported statements; do not turn them into verified outcomes.
- Accept “nothing found” with searches attempted and date as a valid result. Do not fill gaps with invented news, needs, contacts or numbers. Append evidence using stable IDs; see `references/data-workbooks.md`.

### Single-entity deep discovery — dimensions, not company batches
Use `templates/research-dimensions.md`: **one agent per standard dimension**, queued within the recorded agent policy and global/per-tool caps. Assign exact questions, permitted inputs, exclusive output paths and a stop budget. The same method serves **Gate 0 website/UI discovery**, not just outreach; apply it when deep single-entity research is needed.
1. **Brand & communication:** positioning, promise, tone, visual identity and current communications.
2. **Product/UX & customer voice:** journeys, friction, reviews and customer statements; separate observation from need inference.
3. **Business, strategy & leadership statements:** priorities, model, launches and first-person statements with substance/locators.
4. **People & decision makers:** roles, responsibilities and routing; public identity matches remain HYPOTHESIS until user confirmation.
5. **Hiring/team & how they work:** roles, process, tooling, **in-house capability & maturity**, ownership and evidence of existing execution.
6. **Content/marketing & AI:** channels, production cadence, experiments and evidenced AI use (not assumed from style).
7. **Category & competitors:** category conventions, alternatives and meaningful differences, without importing competitors' needs.
8. **Internal context:** our own project files/tasks for this client, **only if access and sharing are permitted**; otherwise N/A with the reason.

All dimensions use the **same evidence schema** in the template: entity/dimension/run/fact IDs, literal fact, source title+URL/path+locator, publication/event/access dates, evidence class, inference/unproven parts, owner/identity status and follow-up. Merge evidence serially; deduplicate by stable fact IDs. “Nothing found” and N/A carry searches/limits, not fabricated evidence.

### In-house maturity — assess early, shape the offer
Review the hiring/team dimension before grouping offers: capability, process, output quality/cadence and current owners, with dated evidence and UNKNOWN where absent. A polished site or job ad alone does not prove every capability. When the team is mature, propose a **bounded partnership adding throughput or hypothesis quality**, e.g. a single-page experiment or a contained production batch; never sell basics they already handle. For partial/unknown maturity, ask about ownership and capacity instead of claiming a deficiency. Carry this assessment into grouping, validation and `templates/message-brief.md`.

### Grouping agent — facts into candidates
After the fact wave, dispatch one grouping agent for the single entity to produce `CANDIDATE-INSIGHTS.md`: **10–18 candidate insights**, each with stable insight ID, evidence IDs, literal fact → conclusion, relevant services/narrow offer, unproven parts, owner guess (labelled HYPOTHESIS), maturity fit and validation question. Merge overlapping themes across dimensions; preserve contradictory evidence. If evidence supports fewer candidates, return fewer and explain the gap; never pad. For multiple entities, keep grouping outputs and the subsequent validation/selection/dossier chain separate by entity, with candidate volume suited to the requested depth. Grouping proposes candidates and does **not** choose final hooks or dossier tiers.

## 2. Validation gate — devil's advocate
Split candidate IDs into disjoint batches for **validation agents** acting as devil's advocate. Before synthesis or briefing copy, re-open **every candidate insight at its primary source** and use `templates/insight-validation.md`. If unavailable, retain the candidate as unverified context; do not promote it to a strong hook. Each verdict retains evidence IDs, re-open evidence and exact unproven parts; agents validate, not select.
- What does the brand actually want? State the literal substance and **at least one alternative reading**.
- Is it current? Compare event/publication dates with today's context and later official updates.
- How important is it: stated priority or minor activation? Identify evidence versus inference.
- Who owns it in-house? Is the team already capable/handling it? Label uncertain ownership.
- What can we credibly offer, and why would outside help matter? Keep an unproven need honest.
- Could the hook sound tone-deaf, exploit hardship, misrepresent success or dismiss their team's work?

Assign **strong / reframe / context / drop**, with rationale and limits. Only strong or revalidated reframed insights become offer hooks; context informs tone without implying a need. A relevant fact does not itself establish demand for outside help. Re-open the source and drop any unsupported need inference.

Validation commonly leaves **few strong items**; that is an expected outcome. Do not inflate verdicts, invent urgency or convert a plausible offer into evidence of demand.

### Orchestrator selection → dossier agent → orchestrator review
Before the next stage, the orchestrator writes **`SELECTION.md`** with setup/version, candidate and validation paths, and **every candidate ID**, evidence IDs, verdict, selected tier and reasons:
- **primary:** validated, most relevant insights with bounded offers; need may still be explicitly unproven;
- **supporting:** corroborating evidence or secondary themes, without promotion to a strong hook;
- **context:** background, tone/ownership constraints and unverified material clearly labelled;
- **drop:** misleading, stale, unsupported or irrelevant candidates, with what not to say.

Record selected order, allowed services/proof, maturity/identity caveats and open questions. Primary hooks must satisfy the validation rule above; tiers are editorial decisions, not new evidence classes. No quota or target count requires a primary item.

Dispatch a **dossier agent** using `templates/dossier.md`; it follows SELECTION **exactly**, including tiers, ordering, omissions and caveats. It cannot promote, replace or introduce insights. Missing/conflicting selection means stop and report. The orchestrator reviews dossier against sources, validation and SELECTION before acceptance; revisions to choices first update the selection file, then the dossier. Explicit chain: **facts → grouping agent → validation agents → orchestrator SELECTION.md → dossier agent → orchestrator review**.

## 3. Message brief per company
When messaging is requested and fits the engagement, use `templates/message-brief.md`: **insight → conclusion → why help → offer → one easy question → what NOT to say**. Insight means the substance of their talk/post/interview, not “I saw your talk”. Distinguish their stated goal from our conclusion; say honestly when outside-help demand is unproven. Select 2–3 services from validated insights, not a generic catalogue; use fewer if evidence only supports fewer. Attach SELECTION path/version, evidence IDs, validation verdicts, permitted proof and setup version. Mature teams receive bounded capacity/hypothesis offers, never basic capability lessons.

On request, use `templates/call-prep.md` for honest positioning, a 30-minute conversation and hypotheses framed as questions; use `templates/pilot-spec.md` for a measurable experiment. Specify one entity/item ID/page, one variable, metric definition, tool limits, baseline, acceptance, stop rules, timeline and responsibilities; include no price. Verify what the platform's tool actually measures; evidence for one metric does not substantiate a different outcome. Preserve proof caveats and disclosure permissions.

## 4. Copywriter writes; orchestrator reviews
Assign drafting and rewriting to a **copywriter agent under the recorded policy**. The orchestrator supplies the brief, checks it and requests precise revisions; it does not take over drafting. If no permitted executor is available, leave briefs ready and report the blocker.

Review each message for:
- insight → conclusion → offer, grounded in validated substance;
- simple, polite, conversational, human language;
- no servile/subcontractor qualifiers or jargon wall;
- at most **one hedge per message**, with honest uncertainty retained;
- one easy question and a distinct job for each touch;
- cleared proof, correct attribution and no unsupported claims.

Use the agreed sample/review gate before mass drafting. Keep message approval and actual sending authorization separate.

## 5. Default chain
For cold outreach use `templates/outreach-chain.md`; agree timing at setup rather than inventing a schedule. Warm leads use an agreed next conversation. Existing clients, inbound requests and internal products have no cold chain by default; generate one only on explicit request, preserving the applicable tone and sending permissions.
1. **T1 — introduction:** who we are + 2–3 services chosen from this company's insights; “we specialise in this and can help”. Proof is not required.
2. **T2 — depth + proof:** deepen the most relevant service and support it with cleared evidence.
3. **T3 — visual concept:** variants made with **their** brand/product. Apply the creative pilot gate in `references/image-production.md`; label speculative visuals honestly.
4. **T4 — routing:** a short routing request to a general company address. Close the prior contact/channel attempt first; do not run simultaneous outreach.
5. **T5 — useful parting thought:** leave a relevant idea they can use; avoid pressure and recycled reminders.

Contact one person on one channel at a time. **Any reply stops the chain**, including a negative reply or routing response; resolve it before considering a user-authorized next conversation. Check reply state before each send. Record touches, channel, recipient, replies and stop reason in the source of truth.

## 6. Cases and proof
- Use only **cleared cases in cold messages**. Keep NDA/regulated/tender work for calls within the user's disclosure permissions; a call does not waive confidentiality.
- Use anonymised numbers only with honest caveats: scope, period, measurement limits and what is not proven. Anonymisation alone does not clear a case for cold outreach.
- Attribute third-party and self-reported numbers to the named source; avoid presenting them as our measured results or causal proof.
- If proof is unavailable, reframe the offer honestly. Never invent a case, imply an existing partnership or promise results from speculative visuals.

## 7. Links, contacts and third parties
Before any agent opens user-supplied links, apply `references/security.md`: strip personal tracking/user parameters (e.g. `user_id=`); if access needs the user's session or permission, ask the user instead of working around it. Give agents the sanitized link, not the personal original.

User-provided contacts override research for routing. Publicly found identity matches remain **HYPOTHESIS until the user confirms**, even when names or employers look similar; separate role evidence from person matching. Record USER-SUPPLIED contacts and confirmation source in project docs. Treat contractors/partners named by the user respectfully as possible collaborators; do not frame them as competition or criticise their work.
