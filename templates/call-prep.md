## Don'ts
- Never criticise the client's team/current work or position named partners as competition.
- Never pitch basics to a mature team, inflate a weak insight, imply an existing partnership or guarantee results.
- Do not present public identity hypotheses as confirmed people or uncleared work as usable proof.
