## Reasoning before copy
1. **Insight:** substance of their talk/post/interview; primary URL, dates, locator and fact IDs. Avoid an empty “I saw your talk” hook.
2. **Validation:** insight-validation path / strong or revalidated reframe / caveats; context-only facts marked separately.
3. **Conclusion:** what that substance implies; distinguish their statement from our inference.
4. **Why they'd need help:** evidence, or an honest unproven hypothesis; in-house owner/capability.
5. **Offer:** 2–3 relevant services for T1 when applicable (fewer if evidence supports fewer); the strongest service to deepen at T2; credible narrow scope matched to maturity/engagement.
6. **One easy question:** [answerable with little effort]
7. **What NOT to say:** unsupported needs/claims, prohibited cases, tone risks, servile/subcontractor qualifiers, jargon.
